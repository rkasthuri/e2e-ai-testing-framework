This document records the dated TD-GOV-001 audit and the operational application
of the constitutional source-header requirement. It does not amend, paraphrase,
or replace the legal wording in `AI_CONSTITUTION.md`.

## Scope and baseline

The read-only audit examined all 709 tracked files at
`12601831ffe0bec34b49b1677eefa3a62a1b7013` on `main`, plus six intentional
untracked first-party TypeScript files already present in the preserved
TD-UI-066A working tree.

The applicable pre-correction inventory contained 440 files:

- 434 tracked first-party source, test, and executable-tooling files.
- 6 intentional untracked first-party TypeScript files.
- 430 TypeScript/TSX files (424 tracked and 6 intentional untracked), 4 tracked
  JavaScript files, and 6 files in the other safe comment formats or locations.
- Generated/runtime directories and non-source artifacts were excluded before
  classification.

## Canonical header source

The sole wording authority is
[`AI_CONSTITUTION.md`](AI_CONSTITUTION.md) Section 3.9. Language-specific
forms change only comment syntax. Shebangs, HTML doctypes, batch directives, and
framework-required CSS directives remain before the header.

The former `scripts/add-headers.ts` helper was not an authority. It contained
line-wrapped wording that differed from the constitutional block and accepted
any ownership marker as compliant. TD-GOV-001 aligns that helper with the
authority and makes exact compliance checkable.

## Applicability and exclusions

Applicable files are first-party, human-maintained TypeScript, TSX, JavaScript,
MJS, CJS, Python, shell, PowerShell, batch, CSS, HTML, and the executable CI
workflow. The inventory is derived from Git-tracked files and intentional
untracked source; it is not a guessed directory walk.

Excluded:

- generated source directories and logged generated test copies;
- JSON and other non-commentable data/configuration formats;
- databases, WAL/SHM files, persisted observations, model payloads, and
  compatibility projections;
- credentials, environment files, logs, reports, screenshots, run history,
  build output, coverage, caches, dependencies, and lockfiles;
- vendored or third-party material, fixtures generated by FORGE, binaries,
  documents, images, icons, and fonts;
- `Forge-Tool.png` and `Forge-Tool.ico`.

## Commentary standard

Comments explain ownership, invariants, failure behavior, security/redaction
boundaries, evidence and provenance semantics, persistence and rollback,
canonicalization, deterministic ordering, cursor/date rules, and non-obvious
accessibility behavior. They do not narrate syntax, duplicate names, inflate a
comment count, speculate about capability, or claim truth beyond evidence.

## Findings

Before correction:

- Exact constitutional-header compliant: 0 of 440.
- Inconsistent or malformed ownership-header variants: 375.
- Missing ownership header: 65.
- Twenty-one verification scripts embedded valuable test rationale inside their
  ownership blocks.
- Six headers contained multiply encoded dash text.
- Three headers used an obsolete ASCII/shortened prohibition form.
- The header helper's marker-only idempotence check accepted all of those forms.
- High-value commentary was already present around core transaction,
  canonicalization, rollback, immutable-observation, credential, and Evidence
  ledger boundaries.
- Eight files needed a bounded commentary correction or addition.
- No ambiguous legal wording, third-party ownership, or generated-file status
  remained in the correction set.

## Corrections

- All 440 pre-existing applicable files were normalized mechanically.
- The 21 verification rationales were moved into separate comments without
  changing their text or test bodies.
- `scripts/add-headers.ts` now uses the Git source inventory, exact
  constitutional wording, safe language positions, explicit exclusions, and a
  non-mutating `--check` mode.
- `scripts/verify-td-gov-001-source-headers.test.ts` derives the canonical
  TypeScript block from the Constitution at test time and verifies exact,
  duplicate-free placement and exclusions.
- The post-correction applicable inventory is 441 files because the focused
  verifier is itself new and compliant.
